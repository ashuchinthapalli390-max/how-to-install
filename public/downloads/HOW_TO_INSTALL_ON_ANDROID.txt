1. ANDROID INSTALLATION OVERVIEW
================================================================================
Unlike Apple iOS, Android allows direct sideloading of applications (.apk files)
with zero fees, no jailbreak, and no computer required!

The only requirement is enabling the standard "Install Unknown Apps" permission 
for whichever app (WhatsApp, Chrome, or File Manager) is opening the .apk file.


================================================================================
2. METHOD 1: DIRECT 1-TAP INSTALL FROM WHATSAPP / TELEGRAM / DRIVE
================================================================================
This is the fastest method to share with friends, colleagues, or testers.

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Send "ChatXa-Android-Release.apk" to your phone via WhatsApp, Telegram, or 
   upload it to your Google Drive.
2. On your phone, tap the received "ChatXa-Android-Release.apk" file.
3. If this is your first time installing an APK from that app, Android will show:
     "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."
4. Tap "Settings" on that popup.
5. Toggle ON "Allow from this source" (or "Allow app installs").
6. Tap the Back button.
7. Tap "Install".
8. In a few seconds, the screen will say "App Installed".
9. Tap "Open" to launch ChatXa!

3. METHOD 2: TRANSFERRING VIA USB CABLE FROM PC TO PHONE
================================================================================
If you want to transfer the APK file directly from your computer:

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Connect your Android phone to your PC with a USB cable.
2. On your phone screen, swipe down the notification bar, tap "Charging this device via USB", 
   and change the option to "File Transfer" / "Transferring files (MTP)".
3. On your Windows PC, open "File Explorer" (Windows Key + E) and go to "This PC".
4. Open your phone's internal storage and open the "Download" folder.
5. Download "ChatXa-Android-Release.zip" or "ChatXa-Android-Release.apk" from:
   https://how-to-install-tau.vercel.app/download
   or copy the downloaded file directly into your phone's "Download" folder.
6. Disconnect or leave connected.
7. On your phone, open your built-in "Files" or "File Manager" app.
8. Go to "Downloads" or "APKs" category.
9. Tap "ChatXa-Android-Release.apk".
10. Tap "Install" (allow "Install unknown apps" if prompted).
11. Tap "Open"!

4. METHOD 3: USB ADB INSTALL FROM WINDOWS PC (DIRECT TO PHONE)
================================================================================
If your phone is connected to your Windows PC with USB Debugging enabled,
you can install the APK with a single command without touching file managers!

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Enable USB Debugging on your phone:
   - Go to Settings -> About Phone -> tap "Build Number" 7 times (Enables Developer options).
   - Go to Settings -> System -> Developer Options (or Additional Settings).
   - Turn ON "USB Debugging".
   - (Crucial for Vivo/Xiaomi/iQOO): Also turn ON "Install via USB"!
2. Connect your phone to your PC via USB cable.
3. Unlock the phone screen. A popup will ask: "Allow USB debugging?". 
   Check "Always allow from this computer" and tap "Allow".
4. Open PowerShell or Command Prompt on your PC.
5. Run this command:
   adb install -r ChatXa-Android-Release.apk
6. The terminal will stream the APK and print "Success".
7. ChatXa is instantly installed on your phone!

5. BRAND-SPECIFIC SETTINGS (IMPORTANT!)
================================================================================
Different phone manufacturers have custom security layers:

A. VIVO / iQOO (Funtouch OS / OriginOS):
   - To install via ADB: You MUST go to Settings -> Developer Options -> Turn ON "Install via USB".
   - When installing, a prompt with a 5-second countdown will appear: tap "Continue".
   - If Vivo Security prompts: Tap "Continue with Install".

B. SAMSUNG (One UI):
   - Go to Settings -> Apps -> Tap three dots (top right) -> Special access -> 
     "Install unknown apps" -> Turn ON for "My Files" and "Chrome".
   - If "Auto Blocker" is ON (One UI 6.0+): Go to Settings -> Security and privacy -> 
     Auto Blocker -> Temporarily toggle OFF to install sideloaded APKs.

C. XIAOMI / REDMI / POCO (MIUI / HyperOS):
   - When installing, MIUI Security Scan runs. Tap the Gear icon in top right and turn off 
     "Scan apps from third-party sources" if it delays.
   - For USB ADB install: In Developer Options, enable both "USB Debugging" AND 
     "Install via USB", plus "USB Debugging (Security settings)" (requires Mi Account login).

D. ONEPLUS / OPPO / REALME (ColorOS / OxygenOS):
   - Settings -> Apps -> Special app access -> Install unknown apps -> Enable.
   - If "Private Safe" warns you, tap "Install Anyway".

E. GOOGLE PIXEL / MOTOROLA / NOTHING (Stock Android):
   - Seamless installation: Tap "Settings" on the prompt -> Toggle "Allow from this source" -> Tap "Install".

6. BYPASSING GOOGLE PLAY PROTECT WARNING
================================================================================
Because ChatXa is an in-development release not yet published on Google Play Store,
Google Play Protect may display a warning dialog:

  "Blocked by Play Protect" or "Unrecognized app"

HOW TO BYPASS IT:
1. Do NOT tap "OK" or "Uninstall".
2. Tap "More details" (small dropdown arrow).
3. Tap "Install anyway" (or "Install anyway (unsafe)").
4. The installation will proceed immediately!

*Note: Why does Google show this? Google displays this prompt for ANY newly built .apk 
worldwide that has not yet been registered in Google's cloud database. The app is 100% safe.

8. TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQS
================================================================================

Q1: "App not installed" or "App not compatible with your device"?
A1: Common causes:
    - You have an older test version installed with a mismatched signature. 
      Fix: Uninstall the old ChatXa app first, then tap Install on the new APK.
    - Not enough storage space. Fix: Ensure you have at least 1 GB free space.

Q2: "There was a problem parsing the package" (Parse Error)?
A2: The APK download was incomplete or interrupted.
    Fix: Delete the partial APK file and download/copy the full 549.7 MB file again.

Q3: Does ChatXa support LiveKit calling and Camera on Android?
A3: Yes! When you first open ChatXa, make sure to tap "While using the app" 
    when Android asks for Camera, Microphone, and Location permissions.
