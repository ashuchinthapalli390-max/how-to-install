1. WHY DOES IPHONE SHOW "UNSUPPORTED FILE TYPE"?
================================================================================
If you sent "ChatXa-iOS-Release.ipa" over WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Drive, 
or email and tapped it on your iPhone, iOS displays:
  "Unsupported file type" or opens a blank white screen.

WHY THIS HAPPENS:
- Unlike Android (where you can directly tap an .apk file to install it), Apple's
  iOS operating system blocks direct execution of .ipa files for security.
- iOS treats .ipa files as raw compressed ZIP archives.
- To install an .ipa on iPhone, iOS requires an installation manifest or a 
  signing certificate profile.
- You CANNOT install by just tapping the raw .ipa inside WhatsApp or Files app.
- Instead, use any of the simple, proven methods explained below!

4. METHOD 2: DIRECT ON IPHONE VIA SCARLET (NO PC NEEDED)
================================================================================
Scarlet is a popular on-device sideloading manager for iOS that signs and 
installs .ipa files straight from your iPhone.

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. On your iPhone, open Safari and navigate to: https://usescarlet.com
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Install".
3. Choose "Direct Install" from the options.
4. Wait 10 seconds for the countdown, then tap "Open in iTunes" / "Install".
5. The Scarlet app icon will appear on your iPhone Home Screen.
6. Go to iPhone Settings -> General -> VPN & Device Management -> Trust Scarlet.
7. Open the Scarlet app on your iPhone.
8. In Scarlet, tap the "Import" button (icon with a tray and arrow pointing down,
   located in the top-right corner).
9. Select "ChatXa-iOS-Release.ipa" from your iPhone Files.
10. Scarlet will sign the app and display an install prompt: Tap "Install".
11. ChatXa is now installed on your Home Screen!

6. METHOD 4: WINDOWS PC VIA 3uTOOLS (SIMPLE 1-CLICK SIDELOAD)
================================================================================
3uTools is a free all-in-one iOS utility tool for Windows.

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Download and install 3uTools on your Windows PC from: http://www.3u.com
2. Connect your iPhone to PC via USB cable and unlock your iPhone screen.
3. Open 3uTools -> Click "Toolbox" in the top navigation bar.
4. Click "IPA Signature".
5. Click "Add IPA Files" and select "ChatXa-iOS-Release.ipa".
6. Select "Sign with Apple ID", click "Add Apple ID", and enter your Apple ID.
7. Check the box next to ChatXa and click "Start Signing".
8. Once signing shows "Signature Succeeded", click "Open the IPA directory".
9. In 3uTools, go to "Apps" on the left menu -> Click "Import & Install IPA".
10. Select the signed IPA. 3uTools will install ChatXa on your iPhone in 30 seconds!

8. CRITICAL IPHONE SETTINGS (MUST DO AFTER INSTALLING!)
================================================================================
After installing via Sideloadly, InstallOnAir, Scarlet, or 3uTools, you must 
grant iOS permission before opening ChatXa for the first time:

STEP 1: TRUST THE DEVELOPER CERTIFICATE
---------------------------------------
1. On your iPhone, open the "Settings" app.
2. Tap "General".
3. Scroll down and tap "VPN & Device Management" (or "Profiles & Device Management").
4. Under "Developer App" or "Enterprise App", you will see your Apple ID email 
   or the signing profile name. Tap on it.
5. Tap "Trust [Name / Email]".
6. A confirmation dialog will appear -> Tap "Trust".

STEP 2: ENABLE DEVELOPER MODE (REQUIRED ON iOS 16, 17, 18+)
----------------------------------------------------------
If your iPhone is running iOS 16, 17, or 18+, Apple requires Developer Mode to be ON:
1. Open iPhone "Settings".
2. Scroll down and tap "Privacy & Security".
3. Scroll all the way to the bottom and tap "Developer Mode".
4. Toggle "Developer Mode" to ON.
5. A prompt will ask to restart your iPhone. Tap "Restart".
6. After your iPhone reboots, unlock the screen.
7. A popup will say: "Turn on Developer Mode?". Tap "Turn On" and enter your passcode.

YOU ARE ALL SET! You can now tap ChatXa on your iPhone home screen and use the app!

A4: Free Apple ID personal certificates remain valid for 7 days. If the 7 days
    expire, simply re-plug your iPhone and click "Start" in Sideloadly again.
    (If installed via TestFlight or an Apple Developer Account, it lasts 90 to 365 days).

Q5: App crashes immediately on launch?
A5: Make sure Developer Mode is toggled ON in:
    iPhone Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Developer Mode.
